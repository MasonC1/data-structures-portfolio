## About Me
I am currently a senior at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, where I am pursuing a double major in Data Science and Sports Analytics. I expect to graduate in Spring 2027, and my goal is to build a career in the sports industry where I can combine my passion for sports with my skills in data, analytics, and problem-solving.
Throughout my time at UNC Charlotte, I have developed a strong interest in using data to better understand sports and support decision-making. My coursework has given me experience working with statistics, programming, data visualization, predictive modeling, and sports-specific analytics. I have enjoyed applying these skills to real-world sports questions, including analyzing player performance, evaluating team tendencies, studying the factors that contribute to success, and using statistical models to make predictions.
My goal after graduation is to work in a sports organization in an analytics-related role. I am particularly interested in opportunities involving sports analytics, player evaluation, scouting, business analytics, or strategy. I want to work in an environment where I can use data to help teams and organizations make better decisions while continuing to learn about the business and competitive side of sports.
Being in Charlotte has also provided me with opportunities to learn about the local sports industry and see how analytics are becoming increasingly important across professional and collegiate sports. I hope to build on these experiences through internships, networking, and hands-on projects before graduating.
Ultimately, I want to establish a long-term career in the sports industry and become someone who can effectively bridge the gap between data and sports decision-making. My education at UNC Charlotte is giving me the technical foundation and industry knowledge I need to pursue that goal, and I am excited to continue developing my skills as I prepare to enter the sports industry in 2027.
